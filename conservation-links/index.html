Conservation Links

Resources for learning more about land conservation.

Below are links to organizations and resources that provide additional information about land conservation, conservation easements, and related topics. These organizations are valuable partners in the effort to protect open space.

National Organizations

Land Trust Alliance

National organization supporting land trusts across America.

The Nature Conservancy

Global conservation organization protecting lands and waters.

American Farmland Trust

Organization dedicated to protecting farmland and promoting sound farming practices.

Maryland Organizations

Maryland Environmental Trust

State agency that helps landowners protect their land through conservation easements.

Montgomery Countryside Alliance

Coalition working to protect Montgomery County's Agricultural Reserve.

Chesapeake Bay Foundation

Organization dedicated to saving the Chesapeake Bay.

County resources for farmers and rural landowners.

Federal agency providing conservation assistance to landowners.

Maryland Department of Natural Resources

State agency managing Maryland's natural resources.
