Our Easements

Properties protected through conservation easements with GSSGS.

Since our founding, Greater Sandy Spring Green Space has worked with landowners to permanently protect properties throughout our service area. Each easement represents a landowner's commitment to preserving their land for future generations.

Below are some examples of the types of properties we have helped protect. For privacy reasons, we do not disclose the specific locations of our easement properties.
