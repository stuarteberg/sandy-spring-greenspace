Protect Your Land

Conservation easements allow you to permanently protect your property's natural, agricultural, or historic features while retaining ownership.

For Future Generations

Your protected land will remain undeveloped forever, creating a lasting legacy for your family and community.

Tax Benefits

Donating a conservation easement may qualify you for significant federal and state tax deductions.

About Greater Sandy Spring Green Space

Greater Sandy Spring Green Space, Inc. (GSSGS) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit land trust dedicated to preserving the rural character and natural beauty of the Greater Sandy Spring area in Montgomery County, Maryland.

Since our founding, we have worked with landowners to protect hundreds of acres of forests, farmland, and open spaces through conservation easements.
