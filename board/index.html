Board of Directors

Meet the dedicated volunteers who guide Greater Sandy Spring Green Space.

Our Board of Directors is composed of local residents who volunteer their time and expertise to advance land conservation in the Greater Sandy Spring area. Board members come from diverse professional backgrounds but share a common commitment to preserving our community's natural heritage.
